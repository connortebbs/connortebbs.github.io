About Me

Connor Tebbs

I'm a mechanical engineering student at the University of Texas at Dallas, expecting to graduate in Fall 2028. My primary interests are robotics, mechanical design, and mechatronics.

My experience includes robotics research in the Humanoid, Biorobotics & Smart Systems Laboratory, mechanical development for NASA Lunabotics, and designing assistive devices through student engineering teams.

Through these projects, I've gained hands-on experience with CAD, engineering analysis, fabrication, electronics, and experimental testing. I'm particularly interested in developing robotic systems that solve practical problems, improve people's lives, and operate in challenging environments.
